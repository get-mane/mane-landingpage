How it works

Set up in minutes.
Paid every month.

Four steps from zero to a predictable monthly income. No booking system, no complicated setup.

01
✂️
Create your plan
Set a monthly price and member discount in two minutes. We generate a signup link and a printable QR code.
02
📲
Customers sign up
They click your link or scan the QR, enter their details and authorise a direct debit. Takes under 90 seconds.

Nothing you don't.

QR walk-in check-in
One QR code on your counter. Members scan it when they walk in. You see it instantly in your dashboard — no awkward conversation at the till.

Direct debit billing
Payments collected automatically every month via GoCardless. No chasing cash, no failed card payments, no admin. It just works.

Member discounts
